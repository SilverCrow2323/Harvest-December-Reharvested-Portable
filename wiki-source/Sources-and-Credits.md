# Sources and credits

The portable project is based on **Harvest December: Reharvested**, a Ren'Py PC remake/port by Autumn Rivers, which is itself based on Harvest December.

## Project relationships

- Portable port collaboration: [takoyaki99](https://github.com/takoyaki99) and the contributors listed by the project.
- PC Reharvested project: [Autumn Rivers on itch.io](https://natirivers.itch.io/harvest-december-remake).
- Upstream portable repository: [Harvest December: Reharvested Portable](https://github.com/takoyaki99/Harvest-December-Reharvested-Portable).

## Content and redistribution

- Keep original game and Reharvested content separate from portable port code.
- Do not upload commercial game data, private assets or content files to public issues.
- Follow the rights and distribution terms of the original and Reharvested projects.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **MUST KNOW!!** A technical port or test build does not grant permission to redistribute the original game's content.
